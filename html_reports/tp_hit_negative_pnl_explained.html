Why a Take-Profit hit can show negative PnL

The puzzle: the reason says Take Profit … +0.73%, yet PnL is −5.94. How can a profit target produce a loss?

2. The answer in one line

The TP trigger and the exit fill price are two different things.

The TP triggered off the bar's high (2999.81), but the leg filled at the conservative session VWAP (2978.18) — which sat right at the entry — so the realized result is ≈ 0%, and after commission it's negative.

  3. Why the VWAP ≈ entry: the price hovered around ~2978 for almost the whole session and only jumped to ~2999 on the final bar, so the volume-weighted average over the session is ≈ the entry (2978).

4. What "conservative session VWAP" is

A running, volume-weighted average price accumulated bar-by-bar over the session (reset at the session boundary). Conservative = at exit, the engine compares the VWAP to the trigger price and takes the side that is worse for the position — it never credits you the favourable intra-bar extreme.

Spec — VWAP definition (execution_logic.html, Formats A & B): 
VWAP = (H + L + C) / 3 × candle_weight     (accumulated over the session)

Why it exists

To avoid look-ahead / over-optimistic fills. A Format-B target fires on the bar's high (an intra-bar spike). You can't realistically fill at that exact spike, so instead of crediting it, the engine fills at the (worse of) VWAP vs trigger. This keeps backtest PnL honest rather than inflated by assuming you sold the top tick.

5. Where it's written in the spec

execution_logic.html · §4.2 Fill-Price Formula
"SELL exits use MAX(vwap, hit) — conservative (pay more). BUY exits use MIN(vwap, hit) — conservative (receive less)." 
Format B:
  SL — SELL:  exit = vwap if vwap > high  else high     (= MAX(vwap, trigger))
  SL — BUY:   exit = vwap if vwap < low   else low      (= MIN(vwap, trigger))
execution_logic.html · §8.1 "VWAP enhancement on Portfolio SqOff" (close-based variant) 
SELL exit:  fill = vwap if vwap > close else close
BUY  exit:  fill = vwap if vwap < close else close

The target side mirrors this in execution_logic_target.html. "SELL/BUY exit" refers to the position side: BUY exit = exiting a LONG → conservative = receive less → MIN(vwap, trigger).

Applying it to this trade (a LONG → "BUY exit"): 
fill = MIN(vwap, trigger) = MIN(2978.18, 2999.81) = 2978.18
The VWAP (2978.18) is below the trigger (2999.81), so the conservative rule takes the VWAP.

6. How realized PnL is computed, and at which price

We exit at the conservative VWAP — NOT the bar close. The bar close (2999.71) is ignored because the VWAP is lower and the conservative rule for a long takes the lower (receive-less) price.

Realized PnL uses the fill price (the VWAP), not the trigger:

realized_pnl = (exit_fill − entry) × quantity − commissions          (long)
             = (2978.18 − 2978.14) × qty − commission
             ≈  tiny gross gain − commission
             =  −5.94

7. Conclusion

This is not a bug. The Target logic fired correctly (high ≥ TP); the negative PnL comes from the conservative VWAP exit-fill working exactly as designed — it prices the exit at the session average instead of the intra-bar spike. When the price spikes through the TP late in the session but the session VWAP is far below it, a TP-labelled exit can realize ≈ 0 or negative.
The lever: it's the per-portfolio vwap_exit_fill (with directional_close_fill) setting.
  • ON (this run) → conservative VWAP fills; pessimistic, avoids look-ahead — what you saw.
  • OFF → exits fill nearer the trigger / bar price, so a TP hit realizes close to the +0.5% target.
Nothing in the Target logic changes either way — only the fill-pricing choice.
