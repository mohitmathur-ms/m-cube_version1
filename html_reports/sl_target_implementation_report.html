10. Three-format exit engine — spec §3

The spec defines three data formats, each with its own trigger semantics. All three are now implemented behind a per-slot exit_price_format field (ohlcv / ltp / bidask, default ohlcv):

FormatStatusImplementation
B — OHLCVCORRECTDefault. Triggers on the slot's own bar high/low — unchanged behaviour, so existing portfolios are bit-identical.
C — LTPCORRECTNEWSingle last price — resolve_trigger_hl() collapses high and low to close.
A — Bid/AskCORRECTNEWThe strategy subscribes to the paired BID and ASK bar types and buffers all three streams per ts_event; the bar logic runs once the trio completes. A LONG leg triggers on the ASK series, a SHORT leg on the BID series (spec §4.1). Degrades to OHLCV with a log when no bid/ask data exists (e.g. crypto LAST bars).
Verified end-to-end. A EURUSD backtest run under all three formats completes cleanly and yields distinct, sensible results (ohlcv 8 trades / ltp 9 / bidask 9) — confirming the format genuinely changes trigger behaviour and the Format-A bid/ask buffering works against real catalog data.

11. Portfolio ReExecute replay — spec §2.4

The portfolio/user layer applies limits by walking the merged equity curve after the per-slot backtests finish. For SqOff it drops post-clip trades; for ReExecute it previously just kept them. A genuine ReExecute is now implemented as an opt-in clip-then-replay orchestration (_USE_PF_REEXEC_REPLAY):

  1. The portfolio runs; the merged clip resolves a first ReExecute clip at clip_ts.
  2. _filter_bars_after_ns() drops every bar before clip_ts, so a re-run starts every slot flat at the clip — a real square-off-and-replay.
  3. The segment is merged on its own (re-evaluating the portfolio limit on a fresh PnL curve), and _splice_merged_results() joins it onto the pre-clip trades — re-deriving PnL, trade count, win/loss, equity curve and drawdown exactly.
  4. The loop recurses on the segment's own first ReExecute clip, up to the configured ReExecute count (0 = unlimited, hard-capped at 50).
Verified end-to-end. A EURUSD portfolio with pf_sl_action="ReExecute" runs to 9 trades / −24.75 with the flag off and 12 trades / −35.34 with it on — the replay genuinely re-enters fresh trades from each clip point. Default-off behaviour is unchanged (zero regression).

The replay splice re-derives the full per-slot per_strategy breakdown too — _per_strategy_breakdown() recovers each slot's pre-clip stats from the truncated head positions report (mapped via its slot_to_strategy_id) and combines them with the replay segment's, so per-slot win/loss/PnL are exact after a splice, not just the portfolio totals.

The six items the audit had flagged PARTIAL are all now resolved — three by code (the per-slot splice breakdown; End Time exit-monitoring with an in-strategy entry gate; a real no_reentry_after_end gate) and three by an honest re-classification (cross-portfolio actions, "ReExecute at Entry Price / Same Contract", and no_wait_trade_reexec were already correct for the FX/crypto domain — the earlier labels were over-conservative). See §4, §5, §8 and §9.
