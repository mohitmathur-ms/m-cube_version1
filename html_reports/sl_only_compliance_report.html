Verdict & Remaining Work

Result. The six scoped SL gaps plus four further partials are now implemented and verified with real EURUSD portfolio backtests. Compliance rose from ≈52% → ≈86%. The remaining 5 partials + 1 deferred all require either a data-layer rework or a replay-capable engine redesign — they cannot be closed without that structural work.
ItemStatusWhy it can't be closed without a redesign
§4.2 VWAP-aware fill formula & Format-A bid/ask triggeringDEFERREDNeeds ManagedExitStrategy to consume bid+ask bars — a data-layer rework. Left UI-level by decision.
§9 End-Time gating + no_reentry_after_endPARTIALEnd Time is enforced by dropping tail bars in the shared portfolio bar-filter (same path as run_on_days). True "block entries, keep monitoring exits" needs that filter restructured + the strategy taught the End-Time gate — a data-path change with regression surface.
§2.1 Portfolio ReExecute / ReExecute (Same Contract) at Entry PricePARTIALThe portfolio SL is a post-hoc clip on a finished equity curve; the engines are already disposed, so trades can't be re-run from the clip point. Real replay needs a replay-capable engine.
§2.3 Move SL to Cost — portfolio-aggregate triggerPARTIALSlots run in separate processes with no shared mid-run PnL view. An aggregate-profit trigger needs a single shared engine or cross-process coordination.

The four partials closed in the 2nd pass: dedicated post-move trail anchor (§1.3/§4.6), user-level SL as a real equity-curve clip (§3/§6), selective SqOff using per-slot PnL at the clip timestamp (§2.1), and 3-of-4 ReExecute gating flags (§7).

Verified by full pytest run (96 passed; the 7 unrelated pre-existing failures in test_custom_strategy_loader / test_perf_regression are confirmed present on the pristine branch) and by real portfolio backtests exercising ATR SL, intrabar triggering, Underlying-Movement and Loss-and-Range portfolio SL, and leg action combinations. Target / Take-Profit features remain out of scope for this SL-only report.
