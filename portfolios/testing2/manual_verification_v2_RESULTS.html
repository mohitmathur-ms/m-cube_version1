1 · How to read this report — three engine facts & three axes

Every trade is checked three ways: when it exited (Time), why it exited (Logic), and at what price the stop sat and filled (Price). We compute each from the data ourselves and confirm the engine agrees.
Technically: verified against core/managed_strategy.py for this build. (1) Entry is a MARKET/GTC order filling at the signal bar's close (no LatencyModel). (2) An SL/Target does not fill at the level — it submits a reduce-only MARKET close that fills at the trigger bar's close (so trigger≠fill, by design). (3) The MIS square-off runs before SL/TP and fires on the first bar whose IST minute ≥ the 15:29 cutoff.
AxisThe check (recomputed independently)

C · PRICERecompute the level from entry fill × (1∓0.005) snapped to tick 0.01 — must equal the engine's SL; and the realised fill must equal the trigger bar's close.

2 · VP-01 — Leg fixed Stop-Loss (percentage 0.5%)   PASS

A long buys and sets a stop 0.5% below the fill; a short sells and sets it 0.5% above. The stop should trip the instant price touches that level, and the trade should close at that bar's price. We check all 12 stop-loss exits and all 14 daily square-offs.

Technically: config delta stop_loss_type=percentage, stop_loss_value=0.5, no target. Long SL=round(F×0.995,0.01), short SL=round(F×1.005,0.01). EMA(9/21) on the 5-min composite is the entry trigger; both long and short entries occur, so both SL directions are exercised.
